Jubilee Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 22, 2026

Overview

1022 Productions respects your privacy. This policy explains what information Jubilee handles, why it is needed, and the choices available to you when using the app.

Event Information

Jubilee lets you save event names, dates, categories, notes, photos, reminder choices, calendar-sync choices, and links to Contacts records. This information is used to provide the features you select. It is stored on your device and, when iCloud is available for Jubilee, in your private iCloud account through Apple's CloudKit service so it can remain current across your devices.

1022 Productions does not use your event content for advertising and does not sell it. Jubilee's anonymous analytics do not include event names, dates, notes, photos, contact details, URLs, or other user-provided content.

Optional Device Access

Contacts

If you grant permission, Jubilee can read birthdays and anniversaries from Contacts for import and can update selected Contacts records when you ask it to export an event. Contacts access occurs on your device and is used only for the action or feature you choose. You can change this permission in iOS Settings.

Calendar

If you grant permission and enable calendar integration, Jubilee creates and maintains a dedicated calendar containing the events you select. You can disable this feature and change Calendar access in iOS Settings.

Notifications

With your permission, Jubilee schedules local annual reminders for selected events. You can turn reminders off in Jubilee or manage notification permission in iOS Settings.

Anonymous Analytics and Diagnostics

Jubilee uses TelemetryDeck to receive anonymous, aggregate signals such as when a session starts, a feature is used, or a purchase screen is shown. These signals help improve the app and do not contain your event content, contact details, photos, URLs, device identifiers supplied by Jubilee, or free-form error messages. See TelemetryDeck's privacy policy for more information.

If you choose to share analytics and diagnostics with app developers through Apple, Apple may provide crash and performance reports according to your device settings and Apple's privacy practices.

Purchases

If Jubilee offers an in-app purchase, the transaction is processed by Apple. 1022 Productions does not receive your full payment-card information. Apple may provide transaction status and purchase records needed to unlock or restore paid features.

Sharing

Jubilee can share an event when you choose to use a sharing feature. The selected event information is sent to the people or services you select through the iOS share sheet. Their handling of that information is governed by their own privacy practices.

Retention and Deletion

Event information remains on your device and in iCloud until you delete it, remove Jubilee's stored data, or manage it through your Apple account. Anonymous aggregate analytics may be retained by TelemetryDeck in accordance with its privacy policy. Removing the app from one device may not remove information already synced to iCloud or calendar and contact data that you previously asked Jubilee to write.

Data Security

We use reasonable safeguards and rely on Apple's platform protections for on-device and iCloud data. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Children's Privacy

Jubilee is not directed to children under 13, and 1022 Productions does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the app's analytics.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as Jubilee changes. Updates are effective when posted on this page, and the effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.

Contact

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this policy, please contact 1022 Productions through your standard support channel.
