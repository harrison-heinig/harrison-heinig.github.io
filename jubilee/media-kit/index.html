About Jubilee

Jubilee is an event tracking app from 1022 Productions that helps people remember birthdays, anniversaries, milestones, remembrances, and other meaningful dates. It brings upcoming occasions, annual reminders, optional Contacts import, Calendar integration, and iCloud sync into one focused experience.
