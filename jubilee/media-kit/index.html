About Jubilee

Jubilee is an event tracking app from 1022 Productions that helps people remember birthdays, anniversaries, milestones, remembrances, and other meaningful dates. Colorful Event Highlights surface what just happened and what is coming next, while notes, photos, search, annual reminders, optional Contacts and Calendar integration, Shortcuts, sharing, and private iCloud sync keep every occasion connected.
