Turnstile Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 21, 2026

Overview

1022 Productions respects your privacy. This policy explains how Turnstile handles information while providing theme-park information, including attractions, entertainment, dining, park schedules, and wait-time estimates.

Information Turnstile Handles

Turnstile is designed to provide park-planning information and does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, payment information, or other personal information to use its core features.

The app may store your app preferences, such as selected parks and display settings, on your device so it can provide the experience you choose. 1022 Productions does not use those preferences to identify you, advertise to you, or build a profile about you.

Park Information

Turnstile retrieves or presents park information such as attraction details, hours, entertainment times, dining information, and wait times. This information is used only to operate the app. It is not personal information about you, and it may be obtained from public, licensed, or other third-party sources.

Analytics and Diagnostics

Turnstile does not include third-party advertising or sell, rent, or trade personal information. If you choose to share analytics or diagnostics with app developers through Apple, Apple may provide crash and performance reports according to your device settings and Apple's privacy practices. We use any reports we receive only to improve Turnstile's reliability and performance.

Sharing and Retention

1022 Productions does not share personal information from Turnstile because the app does not collect it for its core features. Preferences stored on your device remain there until you change them, remove Turnstile's data, or delete the app. Apple may retain diagnostics it processes under its own policies.

Data Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect the information involved in operating Turnstile. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Children's Privacy

Turnstile is not directed to children under 13, and 1022 Productions does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as Turnstile changes. Updates are effective when posted on this page, and the effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.

Contact

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this policy, email support@1022productions.com.
